## Counting and scope

**Before prison: 519 records = 488 model records + 26 groups + 2 separated components + 3 structural variants.** This is not a count of mesh objects. BLEND and GLB are two formats of the same record, not two assets.

Tavern 56; harbor 56; lamp shop 58; additional story props 5; antique shop 93; wizard study 40; captain cabin 26; independent furnishings 6; treasury 17; armory and warehouse 71; farmhouse barn 8; bridge market 83: 519 records in total. Antique shop comprises 73 originals and 20 groups; AT_091 is intentionally absent.

FT_040, FH_002 and FH_038 retain their quality-patched versions. OL_STORY_001–005 retain their approved CC0 versions. Stable IDs and each card's unit description distinguish models, groups and separated components.

## Use

Extract a ZIP and open its BLEND, or import its GLB into compatible software. Place one object and add others to build a scene. Scale, placement and lighting need adjustment; material appearance can vary between applications. Ease of production or identical results is not guaranteed.

See [license scope](LICENSE.md) and each ZIP for the model-specific CC0 statement. It does not extend to articles, videos, example images or completed scene layouts. Completed-scene BLEND files are not included.
