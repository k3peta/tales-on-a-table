## Dragon treasury — 17 distribution sets

[Dragon treasury — 17 distribution sets](catalog/treasury.en.md)

17 distribution records: 16 individual shapes and one gem-pile group. The 25 gem GLBs (five shapes in five colours) are included in the corresponding ZIPs; colours are not additional designs. The scene has 14,167 coin placements, not unique coin designs. Reused assets are excluded from the additions.

## Captain cabin and ship equipment — 26 distribution sets

[Captain cabin and ship equipment — 26 distribution sets](catalog/captain-cabin.en.md)

First publication of 14 earlier models and 12 additional designs. CP006 includes repaired box joints. Reused props are not registered again; two travel bags are placements of one design. Each ZIP includes BLEND, GLB, a render, trilingual README and CC0 terms; completed scenes are excluded.

## New wizard-study distribution sets

[Wizard study — 40 distribution sets](catalog/wizard-study.en.md)

These 40 distribution sets comprise 32 previously created wizard models, 6 new designs and 2 structural variants of the bookcase and ladder. The 397 books count scene placements, not asset types. Each ZIP includes BLEND, GLB, an actual render, a trilingual README and CC0 terms. The completed scene layout is not included.

Choose individual props and furniture from tavern, harbor, lamp-shop and antique-shop collections. Every close-up is an actual render of the distributed BLEND. Start with one object, consider its shape, color and intended setting, and use those details when discussing a new model with an AI assistant.

## Counting and scope

**357 records = 327 model records + 26 groups + 2 separated components + 2 structural variants.** This is not a count of mesh objects. BLEND and GLB are two formats of the same record, not two assets.

Tavern: 56; harbor: 56; lamp shop: 58; additional story props: 5; antique shop: 93; wizard study: 40; captain cabin and ship equipment: 26; independent furnishings: 6. The antique collection comprises 73 original records and 20 groups; AT_091 is intentionally absent. The new armory collection is excluded. Equipment already belonging to the approved tavern, lamp-shop and antique collections remains included.

FT_040, FH_002 and FH_038 retain their quality-patched versions. OL_STORY_001–005 retain their approved CC0 versions. Stable IDs and each card's unit description distinguish models, groups and separated components.

## Use

Extract a ZIP and open its BLEND, or import its GLB into compatible software. Place one object and add others to build a scene. Scale, placement and lighting need adjustment; material appearance can vary between applications. Ease of production or identical results is not guaranteed.

See [license scope](LICENSE.md) and each ZIP for the model-specific CC0 statement. It does not extend to articles, videos, example images or completed scene layouts. Completed-scene BLEND files are not included.
