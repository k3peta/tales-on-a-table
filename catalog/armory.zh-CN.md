# 武具屋と在庫倉庫 — 71単品セット

[素材棚へ](../README.md)

71 model sets: 62 original designs, one fitted sheath variant, and eight warehouse additions. Model geometry and embedded display resources are CC0; previews and scene layouts are excluded. No working mechanisms, LOD, collision proxies or engine certification. Names below retain their Japanese source titles.
