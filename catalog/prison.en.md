The Night Watch — Fantasy Prison
25 prop/furniture designs, 8 primary architectural modules, and 3 connector configurations: 36 distribution records, not 36 unique designs. Each record includes BLEND and GLB. Arranged scenes, generation scripts and example renders are not included in model archives.
Models and embedded materials are CC0 1.0; commercial use, modification and redistribution are allowed without attribution. Article text and example renders are outside this dedication.
Meters; Z-up. Use a 2m XY grid, floor surface Z=0, floor bottom -0.16m, walls 3.2m high. Wall geometry spans local X=0.14..1.86m on a 2m node pitch. Place one 0.28m square connector at each unique endpoint. Floor origin is southwest; wall direction is local +X. Do not double shared walls or pillars. PM009–011 are saved configurations of PM006, not unique geometry. Window sills protrude about 0.105m on one side.
Rotate PM004 Door_Hinge_Pivot around Z to pose the leaf. The example was tested for inward opening through 0–85 degrees. Recheck clearance after layout changes. No game scripts, physics, collisions or LOD. Shackles are static decorative props.
GLB embeds color images, but omits fine Blender bump shading. Appearance varies by renderer. Individual BLEND/GLB readback, dimensions, example joints and 72 door poses were checked in Blender 4.3.2. Large-layout performance and game-engine compatibility were not tested.
