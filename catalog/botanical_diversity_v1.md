## Uses and limits

Metres, Z up, root origin zero. Roots may extend up to 0.003 m below ground. Blender 5.2.2 round-trip checked in production. Stylized thin leaves; static models with no wind animation or rig. Wind behavior, collision, walking, Blender 4.3.2 compatibility and global historical uniqueness are unverified. Append the named collection from each BLEND.

Pea supports are separate. Ivy faces -Y, with the wall plane at +Y=0.025 m; it does not automatically conform to arbitrary walls.

- BD101_Bellflower: Hollow five-lobed hanging bells on branching stems. Flower border.
- BD102_Iris: Sword-shaped basal leaves and alternating upright/falling petals. Garden border.
- BD103_PeaVine: Paired leaflets, spiral tendrils, hanging seed pods. Vegetable garden; add separate supports as desired.
- BD104_LobedIvy: Five-lobed leaves on a vertical branching climber. Ruined wall dressing; front is -Y, wall plane +Y=0.025m. Not an automatic surface-conforming vine.
