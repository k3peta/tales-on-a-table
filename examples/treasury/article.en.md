# A dragon treasury that could make you rich overnight

The existing 17 records have been refined; this is a quality update, with zero new types. The accepted v6 models are shown in a v7 architectural scene lit by warm torchlight. Interrupted mineral deposits connect mortar joints to the floor, with settled dust at column bases and plinth corners. The treasure remains readable.

The dragon dagger now has a continuous head, muzzle and jaw with integrated horn roots and pointed grip scales. Mantle embroidery follows the cloth; fastening details, parchment bands and stitches, casket hinges and overlapping straps have been refined.

The original release, including its 25 gem colour GLBs, remains available. Current ZIPs contain the revised base models. The mantle is a high-density asset of about 4.65 million triangles; lightweight/game performance is not certified and optimization remains a later step.
