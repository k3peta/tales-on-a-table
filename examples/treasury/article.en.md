# A dragon treasury that could make you rich overnight

Beyond the door, coins cover the floor. Gold ingots and gems spill around open chests, while a chain and a dagger hilt emerge from the piles. An adventuring party might need several trips to carry it all away.

![The treasury](ROOM_01.jpg)

This fantasy scene contains no characters. The treasure suggests the moment of discovery: a talisman, dragon-headed dagger and crown in the foreground, with chests, scrolls and an embroidered mantle farther inside the high stone room.

## Magical objects among the coins

The talisman surrounds a green stone with an openwork eight-point ring and a linked gold chain. The dagger has a horned dragon head with red eyes, fangs and a scaled grip. The nearby coin surface is arranged to keep those details visible.

![Talisman and dagger](ROOM_06.jpg)

![Dragon-headed dagger](TD003_dragon_dagger_detail.jpg)

## A dragon stitched in gold

The crimson mantle carries a gold dragon on a navy shield. Filled stitching defines the head, horns, broad wings and curled tail. Fine rows of thread become visible at close range. Leaves and five-petal flowers run along the sides; palmettes repeat across the hem. A navy yoke and ruby clasp finish the collar.

![Embroidered mantle](TD004_embroidered_mantle_front.jpg)

![Stitching detail](TD004_embroidered_mantle_detail.jpg)

## Chests, crowns and five gem shapes

The large chest is open; its contents are separate objects. The small casket is closed. The crown, stamped gold ingot and wax-sealed scroll can each be reused independently.

![Royal crown](TD010_royal_crown_hero.jpg)

Gems come in five shapes: round brilliant, rectangular step cut, pear, marquise and hexagonal crystal. Each has red, blue, green, purple and amber GLB variants. These are colour choices, not 25 new designs.

![Gem shapes and colours](gem_palette.jpg)

## Take one object into another story

The collection contains 17 distribution records: 16 individual shapes and one gem-pile group. It provides 17 individual ZIPs with BLEND, GLB, actual renders, trilingual descriptions and license terms. The 25 gem-colour files are included in their corresponding ZIPs. Reused architecture and 14,167 coin placements do not increase the design count.

[Browse the illustrated catalogue](https://github.com/k3peta/tales-on-a-table/blob/main/catalog/treasury.en.md).

Models use metres and a bottom-centre root origin. These are static decorative assets, with no rig, animation or collision mesh. Material appearance may vary between applications. Coin piles use shaped underfill and repeated meshes, not rigid-body simulation.
